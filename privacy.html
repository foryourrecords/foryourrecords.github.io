Privacy Policy

Receipt and Mileage Tracker (the "App") is a small tool for recording business receipts and mileage. Last updated: November 18, 2026.

What the App stores

The App stores the receipts, receipt photos, and mileage trips that you enter. They are kept in your own web browser on your device and, if you choose to connect Google Drive, in a folder named "Receipt & Mileage Tracker" in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, the App asks for the drive.file permission. This lets the App create and read only the files it creates itself in your Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Your data is sent directly between your browser and Google. The App has no server and the developer does not receive, store, or have access to your data.

Location

If you use the GPS trip tracker, your browser asks for permission to use your location. Location is used only on your device to measure trip distance. Only the resulting mileage number is saved.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. It uses no analytics or advertising.

Removing your data

You can delete entries inside the App, delete the "Receipt & Mileage Tracker" folder in Google Drive, clear the App's site data in your browser, and revoke the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
