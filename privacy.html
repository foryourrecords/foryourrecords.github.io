Privacy Policy

For Your Records (the "App") helps people who work for themselves keep their business receipts, mileage, income and tax records. The App is in beta. Last updated: November 22, 2026.

What the App stores, and where

The App stores what you enter: receipts and their photos, mileage trips, income, estimated tax payments, tax documents and your settings. They are kept in your own web browser on your device and in a folder in your own Google Drive (named "For Your Records"; older accounts may see "Receipt & Mileage Tracker"). The App has no server. The developer does not receive, store or have access to your records.

You can turn on a passcode lock in Settings. It encrypts the records stored in your browser on that device and locks the App after a period away. The passcode never leaves your device and the developer cannot recover it. If you forget it, you can reset the App on that device and restore what you synced from Google Drive. The copy in your Google Drive, and any backup file you download, are not encrypted by the passcode.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your own Google account. The App uses your email address only to make sure your records are saved to your own Drive and to keep each person's records separate on a shared device.

Google permissions

Google Drive (drive.file): lets the App create and read only the files it creates itself in your Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Your data moves directly between your browser and Google.

Team reports (optional): if you use the Team screen, a staff member's report is a file the App creates in their own Google Drive and shares, through Google Drive's own sharing, with the Gmail address they enter for their employer. The employer opens it with Google's file picker (Google Picker, a Google service), which gives the App access to only the files they choose. The employer's approve, reject and paid decisions are written back into that same file. The report holds what the staff member chose to send: receipts and trips with their details and photos, and their name and Google email address. The developer does not receive these reports.

Google Calendar, read only (calendar.readonly), optional: asked for only if you use "Trips from calendar". The App reads your calendar events on your device to suggest business trips. It never changes your calendar. Event details stay on your device, except an event's location when you tap an Estimate button (see Mileage estimates below).

Other services the App uses

Mileage estimates and addresses: when you tap an Estimate button or "Add city, state and ZIP", the addresses involved are sent to OpenStreetMap services (nominatim.openstreetmap.org and router.project-osrm.org) to find the place and the driving distance. Nothing else is sent with them.

Google file picker: on the Team screen the App loads Google's Picker script from apis.google.com. It is used only when an employer chooses to open a staff report.

Receipt reading: receipts are read on your device. The reading software and other parts of the App are downloaded from public content networks (such as cdnjs.cloudflare.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net), and fonts from Google Fonts. Your photos are not sent to those services.

Location: if you use the GPS trip tracker, your browser asks for permission to use your location. Location is used only on your device to measure the trip. Only the resulting miles are saved.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It uses no analytics or advertising. If you email feedback, we receive only what you write and send.

Removing your data

You can delete entries inside the App, delete the App's folder in your Google Drive, clear the App's site data in your browser, and remove the App's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Not tax advice

Tax estimates and checks in the App are for planning and record keeping. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. Check with a tax professional before you file.
